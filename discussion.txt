Highlights of the implementation:
Locates the single-region dundun track automatically.
Detects onsets with Ardour’s VAMP onset-detector and logs each hit.
Extracts 0.2 s slices, calculating RMS and zero-crossing rate for every hit.
Uses a lightweight K-means (default 4 clusters) to form tone groups.
Chooses two clusters as motif tones A/B and builds the pattern
A B B B A B B B A (×4 repeats by default).

Entire process is wrapped in a reversible command so it can be undone in one step.
Open Ardour, load your dundun session, and run the new script from the Scripted-Actions menu to generate the looping motif. Feel free to tweak the cfg block at the top (slice length, pattern, humanise amounts, etc.) to suit your needs.
